## Description

Find your line, one set at a time.

Plumb uses your iPhone camera to review a bodyweight squat from the side. Set your phone where your full body is visible, complete a set, and see the repetitions Plumb could measure. When the view supports it, Plumb highlights one specific pattern and the reps behind it. Try another set to compare the same camera-measured range and pace.

Your workout video is not saved. Derived set summaries are stored locally in Plumb, and you can delete them in the app. On eligible devices, Apple Intelligence can help phrase a measured comparison; reviewed wording is always available when it cannot.

Plumb observes one camera angle. It cannot assess every part of your movement, diagnose an injury, or guarantee safe technique. It asks for a retake when required joints or complete repetitions are missing. It cannot yet verify that every camera position is suitable for a side-view measurement.

## App Review notes — draft

No account is required. The app requests Camera permission when the person taps **Set up camera**. Place the iPhone upright at about hip height with a full side view of one person doing bodyweight squats. The set result appears after **Stop set**; a short or poor view returns a retake prompt. Video is processed locally and is not saved. Saved sessions can be deleted individually or all at once from **History**. **Explain comparison** uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models only if available; otherwise it uses a reviewed local template. No paid feature or HealthKit permission is in this build. Reviewers can use the app without a demo account.
