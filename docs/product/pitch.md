# Finn — five-minute presentation

The current delivery is the [Keynote presentation](../../Design/Presentation/Finn-Keynote-2026-10-16/keynote/README.md). Use its presenter notes for the complete spoken script.

- **0:00–1:00 — Film.** A busy day makes space for one set. A Finn notification, a visible posture adjustment and the phone-to-Watch cue introduce the experience. The film is a scripted demonstration.
- **1:00–1:50 — Problem and solution.** Guided workouts and logs help people start and track training. They do not, by themselves, explain a change in a person's movement. Existing camera coaches are acknowledged. Introduce Finn's iPhone and Watch feedback loop.
- **1:50–3:35 — User experience.** Show the app opening, placement, a measured set, one actionable observation, the same cue on Watch, and saved Progress history. Keep the future projection visibly separate from measured history.
- **3:35–4:40 — Implementation.** Foundation Models grounds the conversation in saved set facts. Vision provides body landmarks. Joint-angle rules and evidence gates select supported observations. WatchConnectivity carries set state and short cues. The standard build uses the on-device model.
- **4:40–5:00 — Close.** A second perspective, a useful adjustment, guidance on the wrist. The next step is validation with real people and devices. **Stronger starts together.**

Slides carry the visual memory; the speaker supplies the explanation. Do not describe the generated film or synthetic demonstration history as accuracy evidence.
