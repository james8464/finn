# Make room — Finn opening film

60 seconds. 16:9. Human urgency, then useful focus. The minute earns the transition into an actual product walkthrough. No voiceover required.

## Character and camera

One fictional man, early thirties, olive skin, short slightly unruly dark curls, light stubble. Ordinary build, expressive tired eyes; real pores and creased clothes. Navy overshirt, grey cotton T-shirt, charcoal trousers, off-white trainers, black watch. Contemporary European city, modest apartment and everyday office. No luxury advertising clichés or smiling at camera. Exercise wardrobe: same grey T-shirt, black shorts.

Observational handheld work, motivated lateral moves, 35–50mm equivalent lenses. Natural window light, believable motion blur, neutral colour, warm skin. Consistent screen direction. Avoid glossy skin, impossible fingers, elastic objects, facial changes, floaty cameras and slow-motion athletic heroics.

## Compositing

Generate people and environments as clean plates. Composite actual app captures afterwards; do not ask the video model to draw Finn interfaces, notifications or typography. Use official Apple bezels only. UI fragments come from the real recording and retain the DEMO INPUT disclosure whenever synthetic demo data is visible. No invented athlete measurements. A reminder is a generic personal calendar/reminder story beat, not a claim that Finn currently schedules workouts.

UI leaves the device along the camera axis, then travels laterally into negative space. Keep a shared vanishing point and soft contact shadow. Overshoot is minimal. Motion blur applies during fast travel, with crisp settling for reading. The athlete footage and app demo are editorially connected but must not imply the synthetic results were calculated from the generated actor.

## Audio

Original percussion-led bed around 120–126 BPM, built around tactile ticks, zipper, footsteps and keyboard. Every hit earns a cut. Silence at 21 seconds provides contrast. No generic transition whoosh on every move. Restrained notification pair, cloth and breath under exercise, short low-impact accent as UI leaves the phone, gentle tonal resolution. Mix with headroom and inspect for clicks and clipping.
