# Five-minute motion direction

The presenter owns the story. Animation directs the eye to the evidence, then holds. All device art is Apple artwork. Text is SF Pro. Product captures and identity are Finn assets.

1. Film: native-format Finn notification, same original sound and human edit.
2. Category: a guided class yields focus to the plan on iPhone at 12 seconds.
3. Reset: the question stands alone, with a short pause before “your movement”.
4. Reveal: icon and name hold before giving the stage to the real launch mark inside iPhone.
5. Setup: device carries over from the previous position. Copy changes when the set starts.
6. Observation: a crop of the native cue enlarges from its exact location in the phone.
7. Continuity: that cue carries across the slide boundary into the Apple Watch screen.
8. Framework: official Foundation Models icon alone. Silence in the visual gives the explanation room.
9. Implementation: Vision, joint geometry, then confidence. Each gets its own speaking interval rather than a diagram arriving all at once.
10. Close: app icon and one retained promise.

Movies use custom cubic-bezier timing, matched device geometry and source-aligned cue extraction. No bounce, camera-spin preset, decorative signal line or continuous idle motion. After each explanation resolves, the composition holds still.
