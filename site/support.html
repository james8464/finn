Support

Finn for iPhone, iOS 27 or later, with an optional Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later). Six exercises: bodyweight squat, push-up, static lunge and stiff-leg deadlift from the side; pull-ups and bar muscle-ups from the side, the front or the back.

Set up a set

Stand the iPhone upright at about hip height, a few steps away, with your whole body in the frame. Turn sideways for the four side-view exercises. Pick the exercise, tap Start a set and allow camera access. Hold still in position for a moment: Finn counts down and starts the set by itself, says each rep, and ends the set when you walk back to the phone or stop moving. Start set and Stop set work too. Hands-free can be turned off in Options.

Understand your result

The rep count and any observation are camera-based estimates from one view. Finn shows at most one observation per set, only when the evidence supports it: a shorter range toward the end, a rep clearly faster than your first ones, or a change in torso tilt, hip alignment or knee bend compared with your own first reps. It does not grade ideal form or certify safety. When the joints were clear in only part of the set, the result keeps the reps and says which joint it could not see, with no advice. When the view is too weak, Finn asks you to move the phone or turn, and says why. Stop exercising if you feel pain and seek qualified advice.

Tap Listen to observation to hear the result. Mark an observation Not relevant if it does not fit your set. Before each set, Finn names the pattern that came back most often in your recent sets of that exercise.

Apple Watch

Open Finn on your watch while the iPhone camera is open: the watch shows the exercise, the live count and the observations, taps your wrist on each rep, and can start or stop the set. The watch does not measure your movement; it needs the iPhone.

Apple Health

Both options are off until you turn them on in Options. Save workouts to Apple Fitness writes each finished session to Health as a strength training workout, with its time, sets and reps, never calories or heart rate. Personalize with Health lets Finn read heart rate, sleep, recent activity, age, weight and height on your iPhone to suggest a lighter day or a longer rest; nothing is stored or sent.

Privacy and deletion

Finn does not save a workout movie. Set summaries stay in the app's local storage and may be included in a device backup. Open History to delete one session or Delete all local data. Deleting in Finn does not remove workouts already saved to Health; delete those in the Health app. Read the privacy policy for details.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera denied: open iOS Settings for Finn and allow Camera.
  • Camera unavailable: close another app using the camera and try again.
  • The set never starts hands-free: hold still for two seconds with your whole body in view, or tap Start set.
  • No measured result: improve lighting, keep one person in view, and keep the joints needed for your exercise visible.
  • No sound: check the ringer switch and the volume; Finn uses your headphones when they are connected.
  • Watch shows a waiting screen: open Finn on the iPhone and start a set; the watch follows the iPhone.
  • History temporarily unavailable: unlock your iPhone and tap Try again.
