Support

Plumb currently supports a bodyweight squat from a side view on an iPhone running iOS 27 or later.

Set up a squat review

Place your iPhone upright at about hip height, a few steps away. Keep your head and feet visible, turn sideways, and use even lighting. Tap Set up camera, allow camera access, then tap Start set. Tap Stop set when you finish. If Plumb cannot see enough of the movement, move the phone back and try again.

Understand your result

Rep count and relative range are camera-based estimates from one view. Plumb needs at least four measured reps before it can show an observation. A measured set may still have no observation when no clear pattern is supported.

Plumb does not yet verify the camera angle automatically. Follow the side-view setup even if it shows a measured result. A shorter or longer camera-measured range does not by itself mean a set was safer, better, or worse. Stop exercising if you feel pain and seek qualified advice where appropriate.

Privacy and deletion

Plumb does not save a workout movie. It keeps derived set summaries on your iPhone; these may be included in a device backup if you have one enabled. After a measured set, Share summary opens the iOS Share Sheet with text only; you choose where it goes. Open History to delete one session or all local data. Read the privacy policy for details.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera denied: open iOS Settings for Plumb and allow Camera.
  • Camera unavailable: close another app using the camera and try again.
  • No measured result: improve lighting, keep one person in view, and keep your hip, knee, and ankle visible.
  • Apple Intelligence note unavailable: Plumb shows reviewed comparison wording when generation is unavailable.
