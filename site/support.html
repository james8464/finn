Support

The current iPhone build offers side-view bodyweight squat, push-up, static lunge, and stiff-leg deadlift review on iOS 27 or later. These observations still need physical-device and specialist validation; treadmill running is planned, not available.

Set up an exercise review

Place your iPhone upright at about hip height, a few steps away. Keep your head and feet visible, turn sideways, and use even lighting. Tap Set up camera, allow camera access, then tap Start set. Tap Stop set when you finish. If Plumb cannot see enough of the movement, move the phone back and try again.

Understand your result

Rep count, movement range, and any posture observation are camera-based estimates from one view. Plumb needs at least four measured reps before it can show an observation. A measured set may still have no observation when no clear pattern is supported. Posture cues describe a change across your own reps; they do not grade ideal form or certify safety.

When an observation appears, tap Listen to observation to hear the rep count, observation, and next action through the current audio output, including paired headphones. Tap Stop speaking to end playback. Speech never starts automatically.

Mark an observation Not relevant if it does not fit your set.

Plumb may suggest turning the phone or refuse a clearly frontal view, but that check is not a guarantee that every measured view is suitable. Follow the side-view setup. Stop exercising if you feel pain and seek qualified advice where appropriate.

Privacy and deletion

Plumb does not save a workout movie. It keeps derived set summaries on your iPhone; these may be included in a device backup if you have one enabled. After a measured set, Share summary opens the iOS Share Sheet with text only; if two compatible sets exist, it includes their measured range-change direction. You choose where the text goes. Open History and tap a session to review measured sets and rep details, or delete one session or all local data. Read the privacy policy for details.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera denied: open iOS Settings for Plumb and allow Camera.
  • Camera unavailable: close another app using the camera and try again.
  • No measured result: improve lighting, keep one person in view, and keep the joints needed for your chosen exercise visible.
  • History temporarily unavailable or deletion unfinished: unlock your iPhone and tap Try again. If a result could not confirm saving, keep Plumb open until retry succeeds; unsaved work and pending deletion retries are lost if the app terminates. Recover history appears only for a confirmed damaged file and keeps a local backup.
  • Apple Intelligence note unavailable: Plumb shows reviewed comparison wording when generation is unavailable.
