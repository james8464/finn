Privacy policy

Plumb reviews a bodyweight squat from your iPhone camera without saving a workout video or requiring an account.

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Publisher: James Durup

Camera and workout data

With your permission, Plumb uses the camera during a set to estimate body position and count camera-visible repetitions. Camera frames are processed on your iPhone. Plumb does not record a movie, save camera frames, or upload them to a Plumb server. The temporary pose trace is held in memory for the current result and cleared when you finish the session or start a new capture.

Plumb saves derived set summaries on your iPhone: session and set identifiers, date, squat rubric version, camera side, rep timing and relative range, analysis confidence, a selected observation if any, and your response to that observation. These summaries are stored in the app's local Application Support area. They are not automatically sent to us or shared with other users. If you tap Share summary after a measured set, the iOS Share Sheet lets you choose where to send a text summary of the rep count and any observation you have not marked irrelevant. It contains no video or camera frames.

Backups and deletion

iOS may include local Application Support data in your device backup, including iCloud Backup if you enable it. Plumb does not operate or access that backup.

You can open History to delete a saved session or choose Delete all local data. The latter also removes a damaged-history backup created if you explicitly recover corrupted local history. These actions remove data from the app on the current device; they do not erase an earlier iPhone backup. Removing the app also removes its local app data under normal iOS behavior.

Optional Apple Intelligence wording

When available on your device, Plumb can ask Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to reword an already measured comparison after you tap Explain comparison. It receives only the two derived set measurements, comparison direction, cue category, and referenced rep numbers. Plumb checks the response and shows reviewed fallback wording if generation is unavailable, late, or inconsistent. This feature does not send camera images or raw pose traces to the model, and Plumb does not operate an AI server.

Accounts, analytics, and sharing

The current release candidate has no account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics, HealthKit integration, cloud sync, or in-app purchase flow. It does not ask for microphone access. Plumb does not record which Share Sheet destination you choose or upload the summary to a Plumb server. If these practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before release.

Your choices and contact

You can deny camera access or change it in iOS Settings. Without camera access, live set review is unavailable. You can delete local results in the app. For questions about this policy or data handling, contact jamesdurup@icloud.com.

This policy covers the app's current implementation. Separately consented research recordings used to validate the product follow a separate process.
