# Finn artwork

- `FinnMark.svg` and `FinnMark-Reversed.svg` are the flat Finn monogram for light and dark surfaces.
- `AppIconPreview.png` is exported from Icon Composer. `AppIconPreview.svg` is a flat vector study. The production source is the layered, editable `../Finn/AppIcon.icon` package.
- The iPhone icon uses black on white in Default, white on black in Dark, and the same silhouette under the system tint in Mono. Icon Composer applies restrained Liquid Glass only to the open camera corners; the monogram stays crisp.
- Keep the monogram optically centered and give it generous clear space. Do not add a dumbbell, a person silhouette, a full camera outline, or decorative gradients.

The straight stem conveys alignment; the open upper and middle strokes suggest forward movement. Two open corners connect the mark to camera-based coaching without depicting one ideal workout pose.
